Mitsubishi Electric Committed $30 Million to an Ohio Cooling Line After the Tariff Case for It Collapsed. Lead Time Is the Only Rationale Left.

Mitsubishi Electric US Holdings is funding a new subsidiary, MEHITS US Inc., to build IT cooling equipment on about 14,000 square meters inside an existing 60,000 square meter facility. Those systems come today from Mitsubishi Electric Hydronics & IT Cooling Systems SpA in Italy. Onshoring cooling hardware had an easy trade case a year ago. It does not have one now.

Feb 20 The day the Supreme Court struck the IEEPA reciprocal tariffs
15% All-inclusive EU ceiling on Italian-built chillers from July 1
Apr 2027 First production, with liquid cooling products a later phase

The duty on an Italian chiller was capped four weeks before Mitsubishi announced a US line to avoid it. What survives is the clock. Fifteen percent on a chiller is a line item a hyperscaler absorbs. Twelve weeks of ocean freight and customs on the critical path is a quarter of revenue on a leased GPU hall, and cooling is the system that most often decides whether a project hits its date. One more thing changed in June: Conserve Ohio filed roughly 70,000 of the 413,000 signatures it needed, so the 25 megawatt data center ban will not reach the 2026 ballot. The group is aiming at 2027, which is the year the Mason line reaches volume.

A sub-atmospheric loop means a seal failure draws air inward rather than spraying coolant onto energized hardware, which is the objection that stalls liquid retrofits in occupied halls. A Seattle Naval Architect Drew a 25MW Data Center Barge. The Cooling Towers on It Still Need a Shore Water Connection.

Hockema Group Inc. has drawn the community-opposition workaround as a hull: 340 by 105 feet, 18 foot draft, 20,000 square feet of data floor forward, 25MW of generation and 340,000 gallons of diesel aft, at an estimated $80 million to $100 million before processing units. Eric Blumhagen, senior principal naval architect, said data centers on land are "burning their bridges." The heat rejection scheme is evaporative cooling towers, which is the line to read twice.

"Wet bulb does not care that the box floats."

The Cooling Report  ·  August 3, 2026

A tower concentrates whatever is dissolved in its makeup water, because the water leaves as vapor and the solids stay. Run harbor water through it and the fill scales within a handful of cycles. Makeup has to come from shore, which puts back the dependency the concept was drawn to escape, and adds biocide dosing and a blowdown stream in a working harbor.

Nautilus already ran the experiment. Stockton commissioned in 2021 at 6.5MW of critical IT load across 20,000 square feet, about 325 watts per square foot, using river water as the outside loop. It listed at $45 million in November 2024. Hockema's 25MW gross puts roughly 950 watts per square foot on the same floor, three times the density, on a tower loop rather than a once-through one.

Grundfos, Wilo, Xylem, Armstrong, KSB, Sulzer, Ebara, Flowserve, ITT, Pentair. Industrial pump builders with century-old catalogs, routed through consulting engineers optimising for first cost and code compliance rather than through the team that owns GPU uptime. A commodity sold that way gets value-engineered, so the pump that arrives is often not the pump that was specified.

A cold plate has no moving parts. Flow is a pump curve intersected with system resistance, and the two failure modes pull in opposite directions. Foul a strainer and resistance climbs, the operating point walks up and left, flow drops, and a cold plate starves. Add racks in parallel and total flow rises while per-rack flow falls, starving the racks that were already there. N+1 redundancy means one pump failure is a maintenance ticket and two in the same window is a thermal event across the row.
